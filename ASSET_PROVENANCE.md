# Asset provenance

## Synthetic PrivacyCam demonstration media

The files in `assets/images/` and `assets/onboarding/` are synthetic
demonstration media created for PrivacyCam. Names, contact details, card
numbers, codes, licence plates, documents, and people shown in them are
fictional.

The July 2026 onboarding photo set was generated with OpenAI image-generation
tools for this project. Protected variants were derived from the corresponding
synthetic originals solely to demonstrate redaction. No real person's identity
or private record is intentionally represented.

## Brand assets

The PrivacyCam app icon and store artwork are project brand assets. Their use is
subject to `TRADEMARKS.md`.
