# Asset provenance

## Synthetic PrivacyCam demonstration images

The files in `assets/images/` and the WebP files in `assets/onboarding/` are
synthetic demonstration media created for PrivacyCam. Names, contact details,
card numbers, codes, licence plates, documents, and people shown in them are
fictional.

The July 2026 onboarding photo set was generated with OpenAI image-generation
tools for this project. Protected variants were derived from the corresponding
synthetic originals solely to demonstrate redaction. No real person's identity
or private record is intentionally represented.
